Why These Fonts

I chose Rajdhani SemiBold for body text to fit the Cyberpunk 2077 theming of my whole page. It has the same squared-off, techy feel as the game's interface, but it stays clean and easy to read in longer passages.

I chose Quantico Bold for headings because it holds a similar aesthetic. It is sharper and heavier, which makes it stand out clearly against the body text.

Fallback Fonts

Headings: Quantico, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif
Body: Rajdhani, Segoe UI, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif
